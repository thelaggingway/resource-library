## Details

### unaligned
- Details: Unaligned is a pay-as-you-go inference provider that sells per-token access to 43 models with no subscription, seats or monthly fee: create an account, top up in crypto from $5 (with 5% added to the first top-up), then use the models from a browser chat or with an API key. Its pricing page lists uncensored GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 variants alongside frontier models, with the cheapest model at $0.05 per million input tokens and most models priced between $0.05 and $2.50 per million input tokens. It is built for developers who want uncensored open-weight models without a monthly commitment, but top-ups are crypto-only and the model names and prices are vendor-listed and unverified.
