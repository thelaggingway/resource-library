## Details

### unaligned
- Details: Unaligned is a pay-as-you-go inference provider that sells per-token access to 43 models with no subscription, seats or monthly fee: create an account, top up in crypto from $5 (with 5% added to the first top-up), then use the models from a browser chat or with an API key. Its pricing page lists uncensored GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 variants alongside frontier models, with the cheapest model at $0.05 per million input tokens and most models priced between $0.05 and $2.50 per million input tokens. It is built for developers who want uncensored open-weight models without a monthly commitment, but top-ups are crypto-only and the model names and prices are vendor-listed and unverified.

### google-ai-student
- Details: Google AI Student is Google's offer page for one year of Google AI Plus at no cost for eligible university students aged 18 and over, verified through SheerID on a personal Google account. The plan includes 2x higher Gemini usage limits, 400 GB of cloud storage, Gemini Omni for video and image generation, and study tools including a dedicated student hub, study notebooks and interactive visualisations. The offer must be redeemed by 31 December 2026 and requires a payment method at sign-up; after the free year the plan auto-charges ₹399 per month unless cancelled, so it is best treated as a 12-month trial. (Coverage note: written from Google's student pages and press coverage — the live page requires Google sign-in and was not directly fetchable.)
