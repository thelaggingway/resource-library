### silk-compute
- Details: Silk Compute is an inference provider that serves abliterated open-weight models — Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, GLM-5.3, GLM-5.3 Flash, and Hy4 — with refusal behavior removed from the weights and no added content filters or moderation layer, through OpenAI Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses, and Anthropic Messages APIs, so existing SDKs and coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Cline work by changing the base URL to api.silkcompute.com. Every model accepts up to 1M tokens of context; per-1M-token pricing on the models page runs from $0.24 input and $0.90 output for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 up to $5.00 input and $15.00 output for Kimi K3.1 Pro, with cached-input rates and a cost-per-agent-run comparison on the page. It is aimed at security research, red teaming, synthetic-data generation, and coding agents that get refused by general-purpose models, with zero data retention (prompts and outputs are not stored), prepaid credits from $5 or monthly plans from $10, per-key budgets and spend tracking for teams, and enterprise SSO, audit-log streaming, and dedicated capacity. Pricing and the uncensored positioning are vendor-reported; a channel reply on the share noted the abliterated DeepSeek pricing is on the expensive side.
